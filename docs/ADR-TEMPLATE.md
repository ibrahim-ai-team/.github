# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owner:** Name or role
- **Related issue(s):** #123 or links
- **Related PR(s):** links, once available
- **Supersedes:** ADR NNNN, or None
- **Superseded by:** ADR NNNN, or None

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and why a decision is needed. Include relevant requirements, security/privacy considerations, budget, and operational limits.

## Decision

State the decision clearly. If Proposed, write the proposed choice and what approval is needed.

## Alternatives considered

For each realistic option, give the main benefit and drawback. Explain why alternatives were not selected.

## Consequences

List positive outcomes, trade-offs, risks, migration needs, and follow-up work. Link follow-up issues where possible.

## Validation and review

State how the decision will be evaluated and what evidence would cause it to be revisited.
